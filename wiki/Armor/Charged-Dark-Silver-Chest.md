Crafted at the [Mjolnir Furnace](./mjolnir-furnace) by upgrading a [Dark Silver Chest](./dark-silver-chest). Part of the **Mark of the Moon** set ([Helmet](./charged-dark-silver-helmet), Chest, [Gloves](./charged-dark-silver-gloves), [Boots](./charged-dark-silver-boots)), wearing all four deals **+15% damage at night**. There is a cape variant that also completes the set.
